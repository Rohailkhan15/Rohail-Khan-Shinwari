I build intelligence into the digital fabric.

Focused on the intersection of human-computer interaction and generative models. I believe the future belongs to interfaces that anticipate rather than just respond.

My journey started not with code, but with systems. Understanding how small components interact to create complex behaviors fascinated me. This naturally led me down the rabbit hole of computer science, and eventually, artificial intelligence.

I spent the early years of my career building traditional web applications, optimizing databases, and designing RESTful APIs. But the paradigm shift brought by LLMs changed everything. Suddenly, we weren't just programming logic; we were directing cognition.

Today, my focus is singular: crafting intuitive, high-presence interfaces that mask the immense complexity of the AI models powering them. I write, I build, and I continuously study the evolving landscape of tech.

What I Do

AI & Tech

Architecting robust systems that integrate large language models into everyday workflows. Focusing on latency, reliability, and graceful degradation.

Writing

Distilling complex technical concepts into digestible essays. Exploring the philosophical and practical implications of the AI revolution.

Building

Content

Sharing insights, open-source snippets, and development logs. Building a community around modern, high-presence engineering.
